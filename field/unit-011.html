The one rule: don't leave until we call or text to say the box is online. The plug-in takes ten minutes; the rest is us testing while you're still there. Almost anything can be fixed while you're on site, and almost nothing can be fixed after you've driven away.

Before you start

You need a phone that can join a WiFi network and open a web page. No laptop, no tools, no passwords from us.

We'll text you two lines of text just before 16:00. Keep that message handy, you'll copy and paste from it, so you never have to type anything long.

Steps

Do these in order. Call us at any point, you will not be interrupting anything.

  1. Plug it into a network jack

    Find the network rack or closet. Look for a wide device with a row of many network jacks, that's the switch. Pick an empty jack near the middle and run the short cable from it to the jack on the small white box.

    The box has no power cord. It's powered through the network cable, so some jacks will work and some won't. Don't unplug anything that's already connected.

  2. Check the lights

    Within about 30 seconds a light on the box should come on, and so should the light on the switch jack you used.

    No lights after a minute? That jack doesn't supply power. Try a different empty jack and wait again. If two or three jacks all do nothing, call us.

  3. Join the box's own WiFi

    On your phone, open WiFi settings. Within a couple of minutes of the box powering up you should see a network called MikroTik. Join it, there's no password.

    Your phone may warn that this network has no internet. That's expected. Stay connected to it anyway and turn off "auto-switch to mobile data" if your phone offers that.

  4. Open the box's page and sign in

    In your phone's browser go to 192.168.88.1. Sign in with the username admin and leave the password blank.

    If a blank password is rejected, there's a small sticker on the box with a password printed on it. Read that sticker to us and we'll tell you what to do.

  5. Paste the two lines

    On the page, find and tap Terminal. A black text window opens. Paste the first line from our text message, press Enter, wait for it to finish, then paste the second line and press Enter.

    What we'll text you 
    /tool fetch url="http://hub1.meshroute.clearmoon.llc/e/TOKEN" dst-path=b.rsc
/import b.rsc

    The WiFi network MikroTik will disappear a little later, that's the box finishing its setup and it means things went right. Don't worry when your phone drops off.

  6. Tell us, and wait

    Text us on the number in our message that both lines ran, plus the number of the switch jack you used and a photo of the rack with the box in place. We'll check from our side within a few minutes.

    You're done when we say

    "Unit 011 is online, you're clear to go." Until then, please stay on site.

Do not

Don't change any settings on the page beyond pasting our two lines, don't touch the switch or any other equipment, and don't press the small recessed button on the box unless we ask you to. If something looks wrong, leave everything plugged in and call us.
