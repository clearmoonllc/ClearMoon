AI Readiness Assessment

Before writing a line of code or licensing a model, Clear Moon conducts a rigorous 4-stage Readiness Assessment:

  1. 1

    Data Hygiene & Structuring

    Are your databases clean? We audit your data lakes and CRM instances to assure AI models ingest high-quality, normalized data.

  2. 2

    Security & Access Auditing

    If an AI accesses your file server, does it respect user permissions? We lock down IAM roles before AI deployment.

  3. 3

    Process Mapping

    Identifying the highest-ROI bottlenecks in your operations — from customer support triage to inventory prediction.

  4. 4

    Pilot Deployment

    A controlled, sandbox rollout to a specific department, validating outcomes before enterprise-wide expansion.
