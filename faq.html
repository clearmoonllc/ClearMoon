About Managed IT Services

A managed service provider (MSP) is a company that remotely manages a customer's IT infrastructure and end-user systems on a proactive basis. Instead of the traditional break-fix model where you pay per incident, an MSP provides ongoing monitoring, maintenance, security, and support for a predictable monthly fee. Clear Moon operates as a full-spectrum MSP serving hospitality, healthcare, finance, and professional services organizations nationwide.

Managed IT pricing typically ranges from $100–$250 per user per month, depending on the scope of services, number of endpoints, and compliance requirements. Clear Moon offers tiered plans that scale from basic monitoring and helpdesk to fully managed infrastructure with 24/7 SOC, EDR, and compliance auditing. Contact us for a customized quote based on your environment.

Break-fix IT charges you only when something breaks — you pay per incident with no ongoing relationship. Managed services provide proactive monitoring, patching, security, and support for a flat monthly fee. The managed model reduces downtime by up to 85%, provides predictable budgeting, and catches problems before they cause outages. Most organizations see 20–30% IT cost reduction after switching to managed services.

Clear Moon specializes in hospitality (hotels, resorts, restaurants), healthcare, finance, retail, logistics, and professional services. Our team has deep experience with hospitality-specific systems like PMS, POS, and guest WiFi, as well as compliance-heavy environments requiring HIPAA, PCI-DSS, SOC 2, and CMMC adherence. We serve clients nationwide from our Miami, FL headquarters.

Cybersecurity & Compliance

Clear Moon deploys a multi-layered defense against ransomware: EDR/XDR endpoint protection with behavioral analysis, email security with anti-phishing filters, network segmentation to contain lateral movement, immutable backup solutions with air-gapped copies, and 24/7 SOC monitoring for early threat detection. We also conduct regular penetration testing and employee security awareness training to reduce the human attack surface.

We support and audit against HIPAA (healthcare), PCI-DSS (payment card), SOC 2 Type II (service organizations), CMMC (defense contractors), NIST CSF (cybersecurity framework), and ITIL (IT service management). Every engagement begins with a gap analysis to identify compliance shortfalls and build a remediation roadmap.

Clear Moon provides comprehensive cybersecurity services: 24/7 Security Operations Center (SOC) monitoring, Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR/XDR), vulnerability management and penetration testing, security awareness training, email security and anti-phishing, network segmentation and firewall management, incident response and forensics, and compliance auditing (HIPAA, PCI-DSS, SOC 2, CMMC, NIST).

Cloud & Business Continuity

Our cloud migration methodology follows a structured approach: Assess (inventory and dependency mapping), Plan (architecture design and cost modeling), Migrate (phased cutover with rollback plans), Optimize (right-sizing and cost governance), and Manage (ongoing monitoring and optimization). We work with Azure, AWS, and Google Cloud, and can design hybrid architectures that keep sensitive workloads on-premises while leveraging cloud scalability.
