Case Study: Physical Security Integration

Securing a 3-Million Sq Ft Distribution Hub: A 500+ Camera Deployment

Duration: 10 weeks

Location: Southeast US

The Challenge

A major regional logistics provider operated a sprawling distribution hub suffering from chronic theft, safety blind spots, and an antiquated analog CCTV system. They needed a complete digital overhaul covering indoor warehouses, exterior lots, and loading docks — all without disrupting 24/7 goods flow.

The Clear Moon Solution

We engineered a robust IP-based surveillance network on a dedicated fiber-optic backbone to ensure zero interference with logistics data traffic:

  • Strategic placement of over 500 high-resolution PTZ and multi-sensor cameras.
  • Edge-based AI analytics for automated perimeter breach detection and license plate recognition (LPR).
  • Localized NVR clusters syncing to a central, fault-tolerant storage array.
  • Integration with existing access control for visual verification of badge sweeps.

The Outcome

Within the first month, recorded theft incidents dropped by 85%. AI analytics shifted security staff from reactive monitoring to proactive prevention. The deployment was completed exactly on schedule, setting a new internal standard for facility physical security.
